What location are you building this guide for, and why did you choose it?
Bandung, Indonesia because it's my hometown and I miss it.
Who is your primary audience? (e.g., first-time visitors, backpackers, families, local residents)
First-time visitors and friend groups. Maybe also families, but mainly people travelling in groups.
What do you want visitors to feel or know after spending 5 minutes on your site?
I want visitors to feel comfortable because they should find everything easy to navigate.
What's one design decision — color, layout, or tone — that reflects your destination's identity?
Earthy tones will reflect the mountain landscapes of bandung, but I also want a more sophisticated tone to reflect the busy city aspects.

Design Intent
What color palette reflects your destination? Name three words that describe the feeling.
An earthy but muted palette. Colors like cream, deep olive, and brass. Three words: Grounded, refined, calming
What typography (heading font / body font) fits your destination's character?
Cursive font
What's one visual choice that connects to your destination's identity?
Not have the design be too loud, as Bandung is a busy city but is very grounded in nature
